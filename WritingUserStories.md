As a seller,
the user wants to share their Product Duel to their Story
so that they can drive more votes and visibility from their followers.

As a shopper,
the user wants to vote for which product they prefer in a Duel
so that they can share their opinion and see how it compares to others' votes.

As a shopper,
the user wants to tap directly on either product in a Duel to view its details or buy it
so that they can act immediately if they decide they want it.

As a shopper,
the user wants to comment on a Product Duel
so that they can explain why they prefer one product over the other.

As a shopper,
the user wants to filter Product Duels by category
so that they can discover comparisons relevant to their interests.

Given the user is browsing the Explore or Shop section,
when the user selects a category filter,
then only Product Duels tagged with that category are displayed.

Given the user is viewing a Product Duel and a product is in stock,
when the user taps the product image or name,
then the user is taken to that product's full detail page with a purchase option.

Given a product in the Duel is out of stock,
when the user taps it,
then the user sees an "Out of Stock" label instead of a working purchase option.
